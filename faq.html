Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our hospital services.

How Can We Help You?

1. How can I book an appointment?

Visit our Appointment page, enter your details, select a doctor and choose your preferred date and time.

2. Do I need to register before booking?

Yes. New patients should register first and then log in to access the appointment booking page.

3. How can I choose a doctor?

Visit the Doctors page to view the available doctors and their specializations.

4. Can I check my appointment status?

Yes. Visit the Queue page to view the appointment queue and sample patient status.

5. How can I cancel my appointment?

Please contact the hospital administration for appointment cancellation assistance.

6. What payment methods are available?

Payment details can be confirmed with the hospital billing department.

7. Can I provide feedback?

Yes. Visit the Feedback page and submit your rating and comments about our services.

8. How can I contact the hospital?

Visit our Contact page for hospital contact information.
